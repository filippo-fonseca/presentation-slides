## Why this framing (the strategy behind the rewrite)

Also Capital funds **"founders building systems that resist decay rather than amplify it"** and things that **"push back against entropy rather than ride the latest wave."** Their get-in-touch line: *"If you are working on something that is hard, necessary, and durable… we would like to hear from you."*

Tendril's enemy is, literally, entropy in a field: herbicide resistance compounds, soil biology collapses, inputs escalate. Chemical agriculture **amplifies** decay; Tendril **resists** it. That is the spine of this deck. Three rules, drawn from how their portfolio (Varda, K2, Radiant, Northwood) writes:

1. **Lead with the machine and a number, not "AI."** The capability is the pitch. The intelligence layer is what makes the fleet compound, introduced second.
2. **Nouns and numbers, not adjectives.** No "revolutionary / seamless / game-changing." No exclamation marks. Never the word "agtech": say "agricultural infrastructure" or "hardware for the field."
3. **State the largest version of the mission plainly**, then anchor it with a mechanism or figure so it reads serious, not promotional.

> Litmus test for any headline: would it sit comfortably between K2's "Build Bigger" and Varda's "Space born, Earth bound"? If it has an exclamation mark, an adjective pile, or the word "agtech," cut it.

### 6 — The system (what we build)

- **section_label**: 05 · The system
- **pattern**: Pipeline / flow diagram (Perceive → Remember → Decide → Act, looping) + per-node detail
- **beats**: 4

**On screen**
- Headline: "Small machines. One **agentic loop.** Every acre."
- Four nodes (reveal per beat):
  - **Perceive** — leaf-level classification at the source: crop, weed, pest, disease, damage.
  - **Remember** — every pass writes to a persistent field map; the system tracks what failed last season.
  - **Decide** — the agentic layer plans the fleet's work against the field's memory and the grower's goals.
  - **Act** — weeds removed mechanically, treatment placed plant by plant, result measured and fed back.
- Footnote (mono): "Delivered through a coordinated fleet of small, modular robots + a live field-health dashboard."

**Speaker notes**
The loop never stops, and it's the same loop whether the limb on the end is a weeder, a UV-C disease arm, or a seeder. That's what makes it a platform, not a tool.

The competitive set validates the market (they've raised real money) without occupying our shape: small + agentic + persistent memory + a swarm that acts.
